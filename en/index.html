Free Brat Video Editor Online – Brat Animator

Brat Animator is a free online video editor focused on Brat-style lyric videos. Edit synced lyrics, arrange video clips and images, mix supported audio tracks, and export MP4 or WebM locally.

Free to use in your browser

No video editing subscription is required to use Brat Animator. Publishing, sharing publicly or redistributing your work requires permission under the site terms.

Features

  • Search LRCLIB, paste LRC lyrics, or use a YouTube link as a reference preview.
  • Edit lyric timing, word animation, format presets, and background media in the browser.
  • Save snapshots, export project JSON, and import preset libraries.

Export

  • MP4 or WebM exports can include mixed audio and multiple video layers when the required browser codecs are supported.
  • Use a multitrack timeline for local video, images, titles and audio; trim and arrange clips without uploading files.
  • YouTube links are reference previews only; imported local media stays in your browser.
