Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The operator and data controller for Brat Animator is oakvx, the project maintainer. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, contact oakvx@icloud.com. This policy covers oakvx.github.io/bratAnimator and its application backend.

Editing and exporting happen locally in your browser. Signing in does not automatically synchronize your media or projects. Optional account, sharing and TikTok features process the limited data described below.

1. Projects and information on your device

Project text, lyric timing, visual settings, imported audio, images, videos, custom fonts and Sound Hub collections are stored in browser storage, including IndexedDB and localStorage. Preferences and recovery information may also be stored locally. These files are not automatically uploaded to our backend as part of editing or exporting.

Browser storage also supports authentication sessions and offline operation. The optional TikTok flow uses sessionStorage for a temporary browser verification value. You can remove local data through application controls or your browser's site-data settings. This may permanently remove projects that you have not backed up.

2. Optional account information

If you create an account or sign in, Supabase Auth processes the credentials required for the selected login method, your account identifier, email address where supplied, session information and associated profile metadata. Passwords and email verification are handled by Supabase Auth. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google and Supabase process the profile information needed for that login.

We use the account identifier to associate application features and local workspaces with the correct user. An account is optional for the local editor.

3. TikTok information and permissions

If you choose TikTok sign-in or account linking when this feature is enabled, TikTok asks you to authorize the user.info.basic scope. We receive your app-scoped TikTok identifier (open_id), display name and avatar URL. We use this information to identify your connected account, display its profile and associate it with your Brat Animator account.

The backend processes OAuth authorization codes, access and refresh tokens, granted scopes and expiry times. TikTok credentials are stored in server-side tables restricted to privileged backend access; the browser receives application session information and the basic profile, not TikTok access or refresh tokens.

This integration does not request private messages, contacts, music favorites or video-publishing permissions. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or sale.

4. Optional sharing and external requests

When you explicitly create a BRATCODE, a sanitized project snapshot is stored on the backend. It may include lyrics, translations, timing and layout settings, but excludes media binaries and credentials. Anyone with the code can import the snapshot while it remains valid. Codes expire after 24 hours and can be revoked earlier. An expired code no longer permits import; database records may remain until cleanup.

Lyrics searches send your query to the provider you select, such as LRCLIB or lrc.red. Opening external media, loading a remote URL, displaying provider avatars or using an embedded video can connect your browser to the relevant third party. Those services receive connection metadata and the information necessary to fulfil the request. Review their policies before using them.

5. Hosting, technical data and recipients

GitHub Pages hosts the website; Supabase provides authentication, database and backend functions. The configured Supabase browser library is loaded from jsDelivr. These providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser or device information and operational logs to deliver, secure and maintain their services. Optional login and media providers process the requests sent to them.

The application does not include advertising trackers or a separate analytics tracker. We do not sell personal information. Providers may process information outside your country; their applicable privacy terms and transfer arrangements govern their infrastructure processing. See GitHub's privacy statement, Supabase's privacy policy, jsDelivr's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy.

6. Purposes and legal grounds

We process account and sharing information to provide features you request. We process limited technical information to operate the service, prevent abuse and protect accounts. Optional provider connections are initiated by you and depend on the authorization you grant. Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, these purposes rely, as applicable, on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in security and reliability, your consent for optional integrations, or compliance with legal obligations.

7. Retention and disconnecting TikTok

Local projects remain on your device until removed by you or your browser. Server-side account and identity records are kept while needed to provide your account, unless you request their deletion or applicable law requires otherwise. OAuth verification states expire after eight minutes; consumed or expired records are removed through the relevant cleanup procedures, and expired records cannot authorize a new session. Provider logs and backups follow the providers' retention arrangements.

Using Disconnect TikTok removes the stored TikTok connection and its credentials from our backend and attempts remote revocation. If TikTok does not confirm revocation, check the permissions in your TikTok account. A minimal identifier-to-account association can remain so a future sign-in returns to the same Brat Animator account. Disconnecting or signing out does not delete your Brat Animator account or local projects.

8. Access, deletion and your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of server-side account information, including TikTok-derived data and retained identity associations, email oakvx@icloud.com with the subject “Brat Animator privacy request”. Include an account email or other identifier sufficient to locate the account. Do not send passwords, access tokens or client secrets. We may request reasonable verification of account ownership before acting.

Where applicable, you may also request restriction, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority, including the Italian Garante. Requests are handled within the deadlines required by applicable law. Local-only files cannot be retrieved or deleted remotely by the operator; remove them on the relevant device.

9. Security and policy changes

Backend tables and functions use access restrictions, and authentication requests use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security. Local browser storage is not a substitute for encrypted device storage or an independent backup. Protect your device and sign out on shared computers.

We may update this policy when the service or its processing changes. The current version and its update date are available on this page. Contact oakvx@icloud.com with questions before enabling an optional feature.
